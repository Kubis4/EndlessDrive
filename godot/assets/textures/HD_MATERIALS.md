# Terrain detail assets

`surface-microheight-2k.png` is a native 2048 × 2048 RGBA data texture. Its
channels independently encode sand, gravel, soil and grass micro-height, generated
with seamless FastNoiseLite fields by `tests/build_surface_detail.gd`. It is not
an enlarged low-resolution source. The terrain shader uses it for fine colour
variation and subtle derivative bump on the actual deformable mesh.

`surface-albedo-atlas.png` is a 1254 × 1254 colour atlas generated with the built-in
imagegen tool. Quadrants: sand, gravel, soil, grass. The tool returned 1254 px
despite the requested 4096 px; it is deliberately retained at its native size.
These photographic swatches combine with the independent 2K detail channels and
the existing fourteen physical surface palettes. Snow/ice and road markings keep
their own procedural treatment. Mirrored tile sampling avoids atlas edge seams.

Godot imports the colour PNG as a four-layer `Texture2DArray`, with a separate
mipmap chain per quadrant. No neighbouring material can bleed into the edge of
a coarse mip level. The shaders account for Compatibility's sRGB sampling when
normalising the swatch colours; this avoids clipping their actual detail away.

`dust-plume-512.png` is an original 512 × 512 soft cloud sprite with noise-modulated
density, generated by the same script. The particle budget remains bounded at
four wheel emitters, with rotating plumes and short ballistic grains/droplets.
Water suppresses the dry dust cloud and throws brighter reflective droplets.
The real emitter scenes are `assets/effects/wheel_dust.tscn` and
`assets/effects/wheel_grit.tscn`; gameplay instantiates these four times rather
than rebuilding their editable appearance in code.

All three imports explicitly enable mipmaps; terrain/rock texture samplers use
anisotropic filtering. The packed height texture has alpha-border processing
disabled because alpha holds the fourth material's data.

## Final imagegen prompt

Built-in tool mode, opaque background, one generated atlas:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: game terrain seamless texture atlas, square 4096x4096 pixels if available, high frequency microdetail. Primary request: make ONE 2 by 2 equal quadrant square atlas with no gutters, no borders, no text. Upper left quadrant: warm tan fine dry sand with tiny grains and delicate wind ripples. Upper right: neutral grey brown fine crushed gravel, irregular tiny pebbles. Lower left: rich brown slightly damp bare soil, crumbled earth with sparse small root fragments. Lower right: green meadow ground dense short grass blades interspersed earth, tiny leaves. Each quadrant is a perfectly overhead, flat uniformly lit PBR basecolor material swatch, each represents about 2m square. Photoreal material textures, subtle contrast, detailed natural irregularities, seamless tiling in each quadrant, consistent uniform ambient illumination, no directional shadows, no perspective, no horizon, no rocks larger than 5cm, no footprints, no tire tracks, no objects, no labels, no watermarks. These are to be used as repeating albedo detail on a 3D offroad landscape, not as a concept art scene.
