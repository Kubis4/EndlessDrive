## Vehicles

The user-supplied `IZH-2715.fbx` is retained under
`art/source_vehicles/izh/`. Its normalized native scene is
`assets/models/izh/izh_2715.tscn`: original body geometry, four individual
rotating wheels, steering front contacts, terrain suspension, headlight markers,
brake/reverse lamps and an offline simplified convex chassis. It is 4.49 m long,
with a 2.635 m wheelbase and 0.332 m tyres, close to the Jeep's overall size.
The supplied file contains no bitmap textures or vertex colours. Native vertex
materials supply paint, dark glazing, rubber and metal without an extra atlas.
Rebuild with `tests/build_izh_rig.gd`; thumbnails with
`tests/capture_vehicle_choices.gd` using the native renderer.

New profiles choose the IZH: front-wheel drive, no 4×4 and no differential lock.
Both unavailable fan controls are hidden; keyboard requests also cannot enable
them. Jeep selection retains optional 4×4, rear drive and a differential lock.
Tyre force and powered-wheel animation follow the actual driven axle.

Garage selection applies to the **next** journey. Current saves keep their
vehicle. Save schema 6 stores the model; schema 1–5 saves without a vehicle ID
remain Jeeps. Garage scrap, upgrades and backup recovery are preserved.

## Road, tunnel and warnings

Guide tyre trails are enabled on hard roads as well as soil. Their bounded
2,048-instance mesh follows rendered triangle heights, including asynchronous
deformation. Hard roads receive compact dusty tyre prints; loose surfaces retain
displaced shoulders. The effects update at 30 Hz and interpolate stamp spacing;
physics remains at its original rate. Deep water suppresses surface prints.
Only the trailing axle stamps the visible guide, avoiding coplanar duplicates
and retaining roughly 665 m of straight driving history at the fixed budget.

Potholes alternate across the carriageway, with a larger central event up to
0.85 m deep and 6.2 m long at full difficulty. Early events are shallower. The
same height function shapes visual and collision geometry, leaving a bypass.

A local screen-space mountain cutaway follows the car inside a tunnel. The road
and obstacles stay visible while the hollow arch and geological collisions are
unchanged. Curved road-edge guides use an unlit material. Low/balanced quality
uses ambient/headlight illumination there; High keeps three tunnel lamps.

Storm preparation is capped at 20 seconds, including restored warnings.
Shelter reachability considers terrain, water, engine condition and current
speed. A storm defers when the car cannot reach real cover within that budget.
Changing the target does not restart the countdown. Engine status and routine
actions appear below mechanical alerts; start failures use the warning slot.

## Performance and packaging

New installations default to Balanced. Existing integrated-GPU preferences are
limited to Balanced once; subsequent explicit choices are retained. Balanced
uses an 82% 3D buffer, full-resolution UI, one directional shadow pass up to
65 m, and no MSAA. High is still available. Quality never removes road collision,
loot, storms or drivetrain simulation.

Four initial particle banks replace the former 24 wheel/surface banks. Other
banks allocate only when used, keep immutable airborne materials and inherit
the selected particle budget. Warmup prepares representative materials without
rebuilding a second collidable tunnel mountain. Balanced uses 16 submitted
warmup frames; High uses 62. The duplicate live-world light primer was removed.

Jeep maps and the RGBA terrain microheight map import at 1,024 pixels; original
source images remain editable. Imported microheight storage falls from
15,912,070 to 4,451,698 bytes. The rock shader no longer loads an unused copy.

`tests/runtime_manifest.gd` rebuilds the runtime resource allowlist, including
native resource dependencies, dynamically named model banks and global art/audio
classes. Source FBX/GLB, development tests, unused atlases and generated editor
snapshots remain outside the exported game. The tiny editor wrapper stays in the
package because main references it; it immediately removes itself at runtime.

The `Runtime Resource Audit` preset produces only a desktop **PCK data pack**.
The old broad resource selection measured 46,245,152 bytes after the texture
import reduction; the focused runtime pack is about 35.5 MB. This comparison
does not include a native executable or Android engine libraries. It does not
claim the same reduction in an APK, and it does not delete editable source art.
`tests/runtime_pack_checks.gd` loads the pack from an empty project directory to
detect dependencies that the source checkout would otherwise conceal.

- The complete Jeep driving regression passed 48 checks: 5,264 m travelled,

  real water and both backward-X mountain switchbacks. Maximum single-tick
  speed loss was 0.428 m/s. Startup/selection integration passed 23 checks;
  model/track selection passed 16, including rendered-height alignment.
- Isolated Intel Compatibility launch: branded title 60 ms, visible menu
  1,687 ms, playable menu 6,216 ms with its cache prepared. A fresh Godot
  shader profile measured 23,179 ms. The earlier complete cached boot was
  8,719 ms; do not confuse first compilation with a repeated launch.
- Full render/streaming probe through the tunnel, 350 m at 36 m/s: median
  16.84 ms, p99 29.59 ms, maximum 33.89 ms; largest publication 4.73 ms.
  The tunnel-specific maximum was 32.67 ms. These are VSync-limited frames,
  not an uncapped FPS comparison or a guarantee for every part of the map.
- All source-project performance results depend on starting environment,
  temperature, competing apps and shader cache. First compilation after a shader
  change is slower than a cached launch; a smooth median is not a guarantee
  that every streaming frame meets the display deadline.
