# Asset provenance and release constraints

This inventory records known sources; it does not grant additional rights.

- **Jeep Cherokee and extracted rig/textures:** Renafox, supplied GLB metadata
  records CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The source, changes and attribution are in
  `models/jeep/ATTRIBUTION.md`. Obtain suitable permission or replace this model
  before commercial use. The repository's code terms do not override asset terms.
- **Chevrolet M1009 (the lead car) and its rig/textures:** snafuj, CC BY 4.0
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/1986-chevrolet-m1009-d2a462c09f054bb093e360a263478732).
  Source, changes and attribution are in `models/m1009/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
- **Active winch:** `scenes/winch_mount.tscn`, constructed from native Godot
  primitive meshes in this project. It has an editable `CableOutlet`. The active
  vehicle no longer depends on the large imported winch GLB.
- **Imported off-road winch GLB:** retained as a source/reference file. Its
  licence was not established by the audit. It is excluded from the Android
  resource export and must not be reintroduced into a release without provenance.
- **Expedition scenery:** native mesh sources are `art/expedition_models.gd`;
  saved editable models are in `assets/models/expedition/`. Image texture source
  and processing notes are in `textures/EXPEDITION_TEXTURES.md`.
- **Supplied vegetation:** the user supplied `shapespark-low-poly-plants-kit.fbx`
  and `CL04_CalocedrusDecurrens_FBX.zip`. Selected meshes and their supplied
  textures were normalized, combined and baked into `models/vegetation/`.
  No licence document was included in the supplied ZIP; this entry records
  provenance, not a new licence grant. Source FBX files and source texture
  copies are excluded from Android export. See `models/vegetation/README.md`.
- **Roadside gas station (Sirocco, `assets/models/roadside/gas_station.scn` and the textures it uses):**
  "Old Gas Station" by CGMeller (https://sketchfab.com/CGMeller), **CC BY 4.0**
  (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: ground plate, road and
  power line removed, merged into one mesh, textures downscaled, box colliders added.
- **Roadside buildings (Sirocco, the other models in `assets/models/roadside/`):**
  "Lowpoly Buildings" by l0wpoly
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/lowpoly-buildings-eccb92b88be04334a8aefd3751612800),
  **Sketchfab Standard licence, not Creative Commons**: usable inside a project,
  not to be redistributed as assets. Check its terms before publishing these files
  in a public repository or a build. Source: `assets/source/roadside/` (not in git).
- **Fonts (`assets/fonts/`):** Anton and Bebas Neue, SIL Open Font License, as bundled in the
  Endless Drive app.
- **Sounds (`assets/audio/`):** copied from the Endless Drive app (`app/src/main/res/raw`).
  `rain`, `thunder` and `thunder_roll` are public-domain Wikimedia Commons recordings (see that
  project's THIRD_PARTY_AUDIO.md). **The origin and licence of the others are not recorded
  there;** check them before publishing.
- **Wheat (`crop_wheat` in `assets/models/highlands/`, `plant_wheat` and `wheat` in `assets/models/crops/`):**
  "Wheat" by KillTheSaint (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/wheat-8d2edddda3104aab8108dffa1bbab6b2),
  **CC BY 4.0** (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Changes: rescaled, texture
  resized, a ribbon texture rendered from it.
- **Sunflowers and the meadow plants (`crop_sunflower_*`, `pack_*`, `plant_*`):** supplied by the
  project owner as `OBJ`/`Textures` and `FoliagePack.blend`; **source and licence not recorded**
  in those files. Check them before publishing. Changes: decimated, sprigs combined into
  tufts, textures resized. Source: `assets/source/foliage/` (not in git).
- **Crops, pines, cacti, shrubs, reeds (`assets/models/crops/`, `pine_*`, `cactus_*`,
  `shrub_dry`, `reeds` in `assets/models/highlands/`):** generated by
  `offroad/build_crops.gd`, part of this project.
- **Lesná vrchovina props (Sirocco, `assets/models/highlands/`):** four Sketchfab
  packs, each **CC BY 4.0** (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
  "Stylised Rocks Asset Pack" by Bl4ckGh0st
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stylised-rocks-asset-pack-d17f102674e84593831f80bc4a451d4d),
  "Tree Animate" by RandyGF
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/tree-animate-f0f9eb5e6c104bbb8e1f41c97019e6f2),
  "Realistic Trees Collection" by Jungle Jim
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-trees-collection-fe67c886eebf4bcb988d7c45e69995ad) and
  "Shapespark low poly exterior plants kit" by Shapespark
  (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/shapespark-low-poly-exterior-plants-kit-de9e79fc07b748d1a6ac055b49ee5c67).
  Changes: selected models merged into single meshes, rescaled and set upright,
  levels of detail generated, textures downscaled to at most 1024 px,
  translucent foliage changed to alpha-scissor; Tree Animate's wind rig
  removed and its original colour textures used instead of the
  specular-glossiness conversion (`offroad/build_props.gd`). The source GLBs
  stay out of the repository (`assets/source/`, ignored).
- **Lesná vrchovina ground textures (`assets/textures/highlands/`):** generated
  from noise by `offroad/build_props.gd` for this project.
- **Other models, UI and audio:** verify their source records before public
  distribution. This inventory is not a completed clearance for every file in
  the repository or the archived Android project.

## Asphalt used by the generated road trip

`assets/textures/weathered-asphalt.png` is copied from the user's local EndlessDrive Godot project. Its `assets/textures/ROAD_SURFACES.md` describes the image as a procedurally generated sRGB asphalt swatch with no additional external licence; alpha stores paint-wear data, not transparency.
