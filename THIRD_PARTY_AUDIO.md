# Third-party audio

The weather recordings below are distributed with the game as Ogg Vorbis.
The two thunder recordings were trimmed, gently level-matched and faded for
gameplay. Each copyright holder released the recording into the public domain,
so the recordings may be used and redistributed without conditions.

- `app/src/main/res/raw/sfx_rain.ogg` — “Rain”, recorded by Wikimedia Commons
  user ジダネ. Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain.ogg
- `app/src/main/res/raw/sfx_thunder.ogg` — “Rain and thunder”, recorded by
  Wikimedia Commons user Caesar in Sundbyberg, Sweden. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rain_and_thunder.ogg
- `app/src/main/res/raw/sfx_thunder_roll.ogg` — “Tonitrus”, recorded by Mysid
  in Southern Finland. Source:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tonitrus.ogg

No endorsement by the recordists or Wikimedia Commons is implied.
