# Privacy Policy for Endless Drive

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

Endless Drive is an offline mobile game developed by KubisDev.

## Information we collect

Endless Drive does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The game does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, location services, contacts, camera, microphone, or online matchmaking.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. This may include your current run, vehicle condition, inventory, distance record, and audio preferences. This information is used only to provide the game and is not sent to the developer.

## Third-party services

Endless Drive does not use third-party services that collect personal information.

## Children’s privacy

Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game’s data practices change. The latest version will always be published at this URL.
