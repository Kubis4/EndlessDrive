# Privacy Policy for Endless Drive

**Last updated: September 8, 2026**

Endless Drive is an offline mobile game developed by KubisDev.

## Information we collect

Endless Drive does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera or microphone data. We do not sell personal information.

The game uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display optional rewarded advertisements. When ads are requested, Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as device and app information, IP address, advertising ID or other identifiers, ad interactions, and information used to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. The information processed and the purposes used may depend on your device settings, your consent choices, and applicable law.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's information about data use in mobile advertising](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## Advertising choices and consent

Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable law, your consent choices, and your device or Google account settings. Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be asked to make choices about advertising and the use of personal data where required. You can also manage advertising preferences through your Android or Google settings.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. This may include your current run, vehicle condition, inventory, distance record, and audio preferences. This information is used only to provide the game and is not currently sent to the developer or synchronized to a cloud account.

If cloud or account-based save features are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are enabled.

## Third-party services

The game uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for rewarded advertisements. Google may process advertising and diagnostic information as described in its own privacy documentation. We do not control Google's independent processing of information; please review Google's policies for details.

The game currently does not use analytics, location services, contacts, camera, microphone, online matchmaking, or developer-operated servers for gameplay saves.

## Children's privacy

The game does not require children or adults to create an account or provide personal information directly to us. Advertising may still involve data processing by Google as described above and is subject to applicable age and consent requirements. Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us using the address below.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the game's data practices change, including if cloud saves, accounts, analytics, or additional advertising features are introduced. The latest version will always be published at this URL.
